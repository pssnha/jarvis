Jarvis — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 June 2026

Jarvis is a private personal- and family-assistant service. This policy explains what information the Jarvis service and the Jarvis Alexa skill collect, how it is used, and the choices you have. Jarvis is operated by its owner as a personal project; the contact address is below.

Who this applies to

Jarvis is used by invited members of a private “circle” (for example, a household). The Alexa skill is account-linked: you must connect your Jarvis account before the skill can access any information, and it only ever exposes data from circles you belong to.

Information we collect

  • Account identity. When you link your account, we receive an identifier and email address from your sign-in provider so we can match you to your Jarvis circle.
  • Your requests. When you speak to the Alexa skill, Amazon Alexa transcribes your speech and sends the text of your request to the Jarvis service so it can answer or act.
  • Your circle’s content. The calendar events, trips/itineraries, and reminders that you and your circle create, which the skill reads back or updates on request.

The skill does not request access to your Amazon shopping, payment, contacts, or location data.

How we use information

  • To understand your spoken request and respond (e.g. read your calendar or next trip).
  • To perform actions you ask for, such as creating a reminder or sending a message to your circle.
  • To keep the service secure and working correctly.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell it.

Service providers

To provide the service we share the minimum necessary data with:

  • Amazon Alexa — voice capture, transcription, and delivery of responses.
  • Anthropic — the request text is processed by Anthropic’s Claude model to interpret what you asked and draft a response.
  • Our hosting provider — to run the Jarvis servers and database.

These providers process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

Data storage and retention

Your circle’s calendar, trip, and reminder data is stored on Jarvis’s servers for as long as your account is active. Conversation history with the assistant is retained to provide continuity and may be deleted on request. If you remove your account or leave a circle, your associated personal data is deleted.

Account linking and revoking access

You can disable the skill or unlink your account at any time from the Alexa app (Skills → your skill → Settings → Disable / Unlink). Doing so revokes the skill’s access tokens; it can no longer reach your Jarvis data.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. Access tokens and sensitive identifiers are stored hashed or encrypted. Access to a circle’s data is restricted to its members.

Children

The skill is intended for adults managing a household and is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.
